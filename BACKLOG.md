# Backlog

Ordered roughly by priority. Items marked *(final review)* came out of the pre-merge whole-branch review; *(UX polish, 2026-07-28)* were ruled out of scope by the UX polish and accessibility design and are recorded here with its reasoning; *(spec §11)* are the roadmap items deferred from the original design.

1. **Finish the release-date curation.** *(curated dates, 2026-07-30)* 178 shoes still carry only a listing year — 27 stamped 2023, 69 stamped 2024, 55 stamped 2025, 27 stamped 2026 — and every one of them can cross a recency-chip boundary, because the listing year runs late and never early (docs/scraping.md §Curated release months). Roughly 70 still have an indexed review and are cheap; the remaining ~108 have none, and are budget models, GTX/GTS variants and brands with no English coverage, so no further publisher sitemap will reach them. The runbook, the publisher yields and the tested Wayback CDX lead are docs/operations.md §Resuming release-date curation. Also worth a pass: 29 curated rows are `suspect`, meaning the month may be off by one — acceptable to ship, cheap to improve if a better source turns up.
2. **Per-column user-declared direction.** *(UX polish, 2026-07-28; partly superseded 2026-07-30)* Roughly thirty numeric metrics are `neutral` in `app/src/lib/direction.ts` — stack, drop, softness, stiffness, every width — because there is no fleet-wide better end, so they take the grey "more" ramp (docs/app.md §Theming). The story scores have settled the part of this that is about a *story*: per-story direction is a score's business and fleet-wide direction stays `direction.ts`'s, so the metrics the score reads are asserted in one direction there while staying `neutral` in the table. What is left is the runner's own per-column preference. Deferred because it adds serialised view state and so changes what a shared link means — `stab=1` is now the precedent for that (docs/app.md §URL encoding), so the question is decidable rather than open.
   **A general metric picker for the score is rejected**, not deferred: most metrics have no defensible direction, so offering one would assert a better end the data does not support; swapping a well-covered term for a thin one *does* move the eligible set, so the list's length and composition would shift confusingly, where the stability pair is the lucky exception that cannot; and it means 2^N score variants to serialise, test and explain for what is one real preference.
3. **Method era: say when a reading is being retired.** *(UX polish, 2026-07-28)* Coverage is era-shaped, and the evidence is in docs/app.md §Coverage: every sparse metric is either arriving or retiring, never uniformly thin. "This test is being retired" is the one thing worth saying about a thin column, and the one thing the dataset cannot currently support — there is no notion of era per test, only the `previousId`/`updateId` links for pairs that were formally superseded (docs/scraping.md §Test lineage). Needs the dataset to carry it. Do not approximate it from the age of the oldest reading; that is precisely what the deleted sparse warning got wrong.
4. **Back/forward navigation, and row expansion in the URL.** `popstate` is unhandled, so Back doesn't restore the previous view. Must be worked through against docs/app.md §View and URL ownership rather than around it. Expanded rows belong to the same visit: putting them in the URL adds a field to `ViewState`, which must serialise, and an expanded row would then stop equalling `defaultView`, so the toolbar would drop its `All` mark because someone opened a row. Both are the same question about what a shared link means.
5. **A filter for the categorical columns.** *(categorical columns, 2026-07-30)* `tongue-gusset-type`, `heel-tab`, `removable-insole` and `reflective-elements` are readable and sortable columns now (docs/app.md §Categorical columns) but nothing filters them, so "show me shoes with a gusseted tongue" still cannot be asked. The shape is a set-membership facet like plate or brand rather than a range — each is a closed enum of four to seven values with the counts already measured (docs/scraping.md §Readings taken from the page) — so it wants `FilterState` to carry `Record<string, string[]>`, a serialisation, and the facet-counting rule brands already follow (docs/app.md §Filters). Deferred because the column earns its keep for side-by-side comparison without it, and because a fifth categorical source is pending: the editorial facts are detail-panel-only today and would want the same control.
6. **Open Graph tags.** *(UX polish, 2026-07-28)* The page has a title and a favicon, so a shared link previews as *something* (docs/app.md §Sharing is copying the address bar), but there are no `og:`/`twitter:` tags and therefore no card. Deferred rather than done because it needs an image, and an image needs a decision about what this tool looks like — a screenshot dates, and there is no logo.
7. **A `side=` URL shorthand.** *(side-as-preset design, 2026-07-29)* The side is derived from the columns and has no token of its own (docs/app.md §URL encoding), so a plain forefoot table is a 119-character link of eight column slugs where `side=forefoot` would be one. That was the accepted cost of having a single encoding of the side rather than two that can disagree, and the shorthand would reintroduce the second one — `side=` would have to expand to `defaultColumns(side)` on parse and be written only when the columns are exactly that, or a link could say forefoot and show heel. Do it if a forefoot plain-table link's length becomes annoying in practice, not before.
8. **One-click return from a table with no measurements.** *(branch review, 2026-07-29)* Untick both Stack and Energy return in the column picker and the view names no side, so `All` clears the filters and leaves the columns alone (docs/app.md §What All does) — correctly marked, and correctly inert. Getting the plain table back then takes two clicks rather than one: a side click appends that side's two measurement columns at the *end*, where `defaultColumns` interleaves them, so the order differs, `All` goes unlit, and a second press restores it. Fixable by having `All` treat "uses neither half" as its own case rather than folding it in with "uses both", at the cost of a third branch in `allView` and a second reading of what `sideOf`'s `null` means. Not worth it unless someone actually strands themselves there.
9. **A first-shoe story.** *(score analysis, 2026-07-30)* "What single shoe should I buy" is the question a new runner actually asks, and no combination of the three answers it — the measurements are in docs/shoe-stories.md §No composite over the three stories, and the failure is that a composite ranks *bouncy and light*: its top 20 is mostly carbon at a median price well above the fleet's, while the mainstream cushioned and stability trainers a beginner is usually pointed at sit in the bottom third. The gap is a story rather than a statistic, so it arrives as a fourth `ScoreDef` (docs/app.md §The story scores) built on terms the three under-use — stack, midsole width, heel counter, outsole life — and deliberately not on energy return. Two things it must settle before any weight is chosen: the pool its frozen constants are derived over (docs/decisions.md §Frozen scores and live thresholds), for which *in production and unplated* is the candidate, and how it survives the honesty limit that fit, upper and comfort are most of what makes a first shoe good and the fleet measures none of them — so it must not claim to rank "best". Worth knowing first: the useful list this was reaching for already exists without a new score, as the plain filters *unplated, at or below the fleet's median price, in production* over any of the three columns — read the median off the price filter rather than hard-coding one, since it moves with every refresh.
10. **Head-to-head compare view.** *(spec §11)* Select 2–4 shoes for a side-by-side metric comparison.
11. **Other categories.** *(spec §11)* Trail/hiking/sneakers — the pipeline is parameterised by seed shoe already; needs a category switcher in the UI and per-category datasets. Each details record already carries the per-shoe `categorySlug` this would key on (docs/scraping.md §Non-running shoes), which currently only ever excludes.
12. **Price/deal tracking.** *(spec §11)* MSRP history is implicit in git history of `data/`; surface it.
